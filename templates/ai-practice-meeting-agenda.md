# AI practice meeting: 30-minute agenda

Whole team, same slot, weekly for the first month and then every other week. The lead goes first.
A rotating host owns the agenda. One person (the librarian) keeps the shared skill library current.

| Minutes | Item |
|---|---|
| 0-3 | Win or flop of the week, lead first |
| 3-15 | Two 5-minute demos of real work: the task, the skill or prompt, the output, what you had to fix. Every other session, one demo is a failure. |
| 15-22 | Rework goes upstream: pick one correction and decide where it lives (a skill, a goal, a checkpoint) |
| 22-27 | Decision rules: what is allowed, what needs a human checkpoint, what is a two-way door |
| 27-30 | One thing each person tries this week |

## Keep after every session

- The shared skill library (name, version, owner, when not to use it)
- The decision register (see `skills/checkpoint-designer/decision-register-template.md`)
- A short list of what AI does well, struggles with and fails at, for your team's work
