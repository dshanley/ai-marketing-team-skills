# Card A: Team lead

**Your team of 4 is actually 40.** Anyone running an agent is managing one. Your job is to set the
standard so they manage it well.

## Before Monday

- [ ] List your 40 across four rings: your own assistants, your team's assistants, your vendors' agents, and your heaviest AI user (really ten people with no manager training).
- [ ] Book the 60-minute kickoff for your AI practice meeting.

## The loop, per team project

| # | Practice | Your move |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Outcome, not solution** | Brief the team in outcomes. Ask them to brief their agents the same way. |
| 02 | **Checkpoint every step** | Agree the yes/no checks for each recurring workflow, and who answers each one. A two-way door (cheap to undo) is decided by them or their agent. A one-way door comes to you as problem, options, recommendation. |
| 03 | **Write the goal down** | Write the team goal. Keep the three-sentence task brief (customer, user, purpose) in the shared folder. |
| 04 | **Define good** | Review closely until you can write it down. The written process becomes a shared, versioned skill with an owner. |
| 05 | **Pre-mortem, then small** | Run one before anything consequential. Ship the smallest useful piece first. |
| 06 | **One bottleneck** | The team picks one each cycle and goes all the way down on it. |

Then go back to 01 on the next project. Step 04 feeds 02 and 03: every correction you push upstream
makes the next run need fewer.

## Every week or two: the 30-minute AI practice meeting

- Win or flop of the week, you first (3 min).
- Two 5-minute demos of real work. Every other session, one is a failure (12 min).
- Pick one correction and decide where it goes: a skill, a goal or a checkpoint (7 min).
- One decision rule: what is allowed, what needs a human, what is a two-way door (5 min).
- One thing each person tries this week (3 min).

## Watch

- Who demoed or used a shared skill this fortnight.
- How many recurring deliverables have a written process.
- Whether anyone is holding back on showing their AI use. Ask: "Did you hold back on trying or showing AI this month?"
