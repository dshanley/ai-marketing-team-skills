## Three-sentence task brief

1. **Customer:** who pays for or is helped by the outcome.
2. **User:** who uses the work product day to day.
3. **Purpose:** what the work is for, and what changes if it works.

## Load order for an agent

Company, then department or client, then current state (what has already happened), then the process
(numbered steps with a check at each), then the task. Load only what the task needs.
